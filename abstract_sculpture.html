Abstract Sculpture

Product Description:

This item is handmade in Colorado out of 1.5" x 1.5" 1/8" thick aluminum square tubing. These can be used indoors or outdoors. There are no bolts, and it is welded into one piece.

This item is made out of raw marine grade aluminum. It will not rust, and it is very resistant to corrosion. For example, many boats are made out of raw aluminum. The rough grinded finish will allow scratches on the soft aluminum to blend over time. We prefer this simplicity over worrying about scratches in paint.

We recommend renaissance wax for protecting all our raw metal products indoors and outdoors. This wax was designed by UK museums to protect many of their items, and it is known to enhance the appearance of the item. It is extremely easy to apply, and there is no mess or dangerous fumes. Simply wipe it on with a cloth (it only takes a very thin layer). In 15 minutes it will cure into an invisible, hard protective layer. Multiple coats should be applied for additional durability. When used outdoors, this wax is typically applied 1-2 times per year, depending on climate and handling. When used indoors, it can last much longer. However, high contact areas may require reapplication every few months. Some level of corrosion may still be experienced.
