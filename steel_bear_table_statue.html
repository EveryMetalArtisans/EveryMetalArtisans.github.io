Steel Bear Table/Statue

This is a sculpture of a bear that can be used as a side table. It is about 19 inches tall, 40 inches long, and 16 inches. It is made of mild steel. It weighs about 55 lbs. It is treated with renaissance wax. This is the wax that museums use to preserve most things. It cures to a very thin, invisible, and hard layer.

It is still mild steel, and it may develop some rust or a natural patina. Don't overthink it, this is just part of life.
