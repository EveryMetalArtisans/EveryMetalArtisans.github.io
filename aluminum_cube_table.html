Aluminum Cube Table

Product Description:

Handmade in Colorado out of 3/16" thick aluminum plate. These cubes can be made any size and they are light and strong. They can be used indoors or outdoors. There are no bolts, and they are welded into one piece. They are completely enclosed, besides small weep holes in the bottom to allow moisture from condensation to escape. The weep holes need to face downward. These cubes can be used as side tables, end tables, coffee tables, nightstands, dining tables, console tables, and bars. Rectangular cubes are an option that allows for longer or taller tables. Rectangular prisms make a good bar to serve and enjoy drinks.

For rectangular cubes and prisms, it is important to note the orientation in which you wish to use them. Otherwise, the weep holes could be on a side that you do not plan to use as the bottom. If you request a size that there is not currently a listing for, as described below, please include the dimensions of the face where you want the weep holes.

Free shipping to the mainland USA!!!

Optional Coatings:

These can be professionally painted or powder coated for an additional charge.

However, we prefer the raw aluminum. It will not rust, and the rough grinded finish will allow scratches on the soft aluminum to blend over time. We prefer this simplicity over worrying about scratches in paint. We recommend renaissance wax for protecting all our raw metal products in mild to moderately corrosive environments. Simply wipe it on with a cloth (it only takes a very thin layer). In 15 minutes it will cure into a invisible, hard protective layer. This wax was designed by UK museums to protect many of their items. It is the gold standard of this type of product and worth every penny compared to others. Finally, this wax is known to enhance the color, luster and other visual aspects of the item. Some level of corrosion may still be experienced.

Corrosion Mitigation:

Depending on your climate, the raw aluminum may develop a white chalky surface over time due to corrosion. This can be periodically addressed with an aluminum restoration solution. In addition, further corrosion such as pitting may be experienced in humid and/or salty coastal environments. This can be mitigated to varying degrees with primer, paint, Penetrol, Permalac, powder coat, etc.. However, it is extremely common to use raw aluminum outdoors. For example, you can see raw aluminum on trucks and trailers on the road every day.

Another great option is to use a two part can of spray paint, or even any spray paint (clear with no primer is an acceptable option, but you should use a paint that is designed for direct to metal application). This allows you to always have a can around, and if it is scratched you can sand it and fix it. Additionally, when the paint eventually needs to be redone, after however many years, it is very easy to quickly sand it and respray it.

Steel Version:

All of our products can also be made out of mild steel for a 20% lower price. These will rust and all of the same coatings can be applied. Raw steel is a viable option indoors and out. In dry climates it takes a very very long time for steel to rust to a point where the structure begins to fail (potentially 100+ years). The rate of corrosion is likely only a concern if the item is used raw outdoors in very humid and/or salty coastal environments. In mildly humid environments, rust still takes quite a while to cause structural failure, and low effort coatings can extend this significantly. Further coatings of interest include Rustoleum spray paints, cold galv spray, and rust converter.
